# Source notices

The analysis code accompanies *Causal effect of prolonged continuous renal replacement therapy on mortality of patients in the ICU: A retrospective cohort study*.

Sensitivity calculations follow VanderWeele and Ding and Chernozhukov and colleagues. DoubleML supplies the reference sensitivity calculation. CATE uses EconML XLearner. The frameworks are dependencies and are not copied into this repository.

Clinical records and research outputs are not distributed with this code.
